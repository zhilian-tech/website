What we make

Our core is precise, efficient induction heating paired with software we write ourselves. We build it into finished equipment, or into components for your product.

  • Induction heating systems

    Coils, power boards and control logic tuned for fast, even heat and stable output under continuous use.

  • Connected controls

    Embedded firmware, app and cloud connectivity, remote monitoring and over-the-air updates. We hold six registered software copyrights.

  • Commercial cooking appliances

    Countertop equipment for restaurants, convenience stores and self-service venues, designed for long shifts and easy cleaning.

  • Custom smart appliances

    New categories built on the same heating and control platform, from first concept to mass production.

OEM and ODM manufacturing

Bring a finished design for us to produce, or bring an idea and we develop it with you. One team handles engineering, certification support and production.

  1. Brief

    You share the product, target market and price point.

  2. Engineering

    We design or adapt the hardware, firmware and housing.

  3. Prototypes

    Working samples for your testing and feedback.

  4. Certification

    We prepare the product and documents for your market's requirements.

  5. Production

    Dedicated lines, incoming and final inspection, export packing.

Minimum order, lead time and pricing depend on the project. Tell us about yours and we will reply with a proposal.
